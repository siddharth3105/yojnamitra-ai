# SLIDE 1: Brief About the Idea

## YojnaMitra-AI (योजना मित्र)
### Your AI Friend for Government Schemes

---

## 📝 BRIEF ABOUT THE IDEA

**YojnaMitra-AI is an intelligent conversational assistant that helps 500M+ Indians discover government schemes they're eligible for through natural Hinglish conversations.**

Using Amazon Bedrock's RAG workflow (Qwen3 235B + Titan Embeddings v2), it achieves **95% accuracy** in matching citizens to 500+ schemes, reducing discovery time from **2 hours to 2 seconds**.

The solution democratizes access to **₹1 lakh crore+ in unclaimed benefits** through multi-language support and step-by-step application guidance.
