## RECORDING TIPS

### Voice Quality
- Use a good quality microphone (USB mic or smartphone with good audio)
- Record in a quiet room with minimal echo
- Speak clearly and maintain consistent volume
- Add slight pauses between scenes for natural flow

### Tone Guidelines
- **Introduction**: Warm and welcoming
- **Problem**: Concerned but not negative
- **Solution**: Excited and confident
- **Demo**: Explanatory and clear
- **Conclusion**: Inspiring and proud

### Emphasis Points
- Emphasize numbers: "500 schemes", "70% of citizens", "12 languages", "2 rupees per month"
- Emphasize benefits: "lakhs of rupees", "step by step", "natural conversation"
- Emphasize technology: "Amazon Bedrock", "Qwen3 235B", "RAG technology"

### Pacing
- Slow down for technical terms (Amazon Bedrock, Retrieval Augmented Generation)
- Speed up slightly for conversational parts
- Pause for 1-2 seconds between major scenes
- Pause for 0.5 seconds after key statistics

### Hindi Pronunciation
- Namaste: Nuh-muh-stay
- Lakhs: Lacks (rhymes with "backs")
- Crore: Kror (rhymes with "more")
- Yojana: Yo-juh-naa
